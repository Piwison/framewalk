P0 1 · Diary import no schema change

Bring a diary back from a file.

Settings already lets you export. This is the other half of the promise: restore. It merges gently — add what’s new, skip what’s already here — so there’s never a scary “overwrite?” dialog. Nothing is destroyed, so nothing needs confirming.

Your call: merge-by-id only (recommended) — or also a “replace my whole diary” option? I’d hold replace-all for later.

The four states

Click Restore from a file to cycle through them — idle → reading → restored → already-up-to-date → a bad file. Success is marked with a quiet full stop, not a celebration; a bad file declines calmly and changes nothing.

Merge-by-id makes re-importing the same file a no-op. Images ride in as the same data: URLs export writes, converted back to on-device blobs. No network, ever — the CSP already forbids it.

P1 2 · Rolls Phase 2 — pick cover no migration

Let the best frame lead the roll.

A roll saves with the first kept frame as its cover — but the strongest frame isn’t always first. Open a roll’s grid and tap Make cover on any frame. The one wearing the amber Cover tag is the current cover.

Your call: cover-pick only (recommended). Reorder means drag handles — the “editor chrome” Ma avoids — so I’d park it.

What I’m recommending we don’t build now.

Rolls: add-frames / multiple rolls per walk
New editing + multi-select surface against a rarely-hit need. Hold the Phase 1 line.
APG arrow-key nav for radiogroups
AA already met via Tab. Fold into any future radiogroup change, not its own push.
AI — mission enrichment · cull assist · PoseCue
Gated on “loop loved first.” No signal yet; each adds deps, cost, and privacy surface. Needs your sign-off.
End-to-end encrypted sync
Accounts + backend + breaks “$0, no accounts.” Diary import covers ~80% of the felt need at ~5% of the cost.
Zine / print export
On-thesis, but a reward after the habit, not a driver of it. Strong v1.3.
Group “walk together”
Reintroduces the comparison surface the product exists to avoid. High taste-risk.
